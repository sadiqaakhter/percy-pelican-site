Meet the Author

Marc P. Silvestri

Marc P. Silvestri is the creator of the Percy Pelican series, the Just Right For Me books, and other stories for preschoolers. His children's books focus on courage, kindness, self-worth, confidence, compassion, and resilience.

Through memorable characters and gentle adventures, Marc creates stories that help young readers feel seen, supported, and brave enough to keep trying.
