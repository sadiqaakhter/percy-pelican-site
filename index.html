Meet Percy Pelican

Percy is a fluffy white pelican from Pelican Bay. He is gentle, determined, and kind. At first, Percy feels discouraged because catching fish is hard for him. But once he receives his glasses, he discovers that seeing clearly helps him become more confident and brave.

GentleCuriousDeterminedBraveHelpful

Meet Pip Pelican

Pip is Percy's young pelican friend from Pelican Bay. He is curious, playful, and full of energy. Sometimes Pip gets nervous or unsure, but with Percy's support, he learns to be brave, trust himself, and enjoy new adventures. Pip represents friendship, courage, and the joy of learning with someone who cares.

CuriousPlayfulBraveCheerfulTrusting

Meet Crab

Crab is Percy's helpful friend. When Percy feels sad, Crab listens, cares, and gives him the glasses that help change his day. Crab represents kindness, friendship, and the power of helping someone when they need it most.

KindHelpfulFunnySupportive

Meet the Author

Marc P. Silvestri

Marc P. Silvestri is the creator of the Percy Pelican series, the Just Right For Me books, and other stories for preschoolers. His children's books celebrate courage, kindness, self-worth, and finding your place in the world. Through memorable characters and gentle adventures, Marc creates stories that encourage confidence, compassion, and resilience in young readers.
